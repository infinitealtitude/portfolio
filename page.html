A little about me

As a child, I took apart toys, VCRs and CD players, usually with more enthusiasm for taking them apart than putting them back together.

That curiosity led me to computer science, software development and consulting. Over more than 14 years, my work across India, Australia and the UK has taken me through engineering, enterprise AI and commercial strategy.

I still like getting my hands into the code. I want to understand how something works and why it matters to the people who will use or buy it.
